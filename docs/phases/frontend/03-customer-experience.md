# Phase 17 — Customer Experience, Wishlist & About Policies

## Objective
Deliver a unified, responsive customer shopping journey across multiple independent artisan studios.

## Implemented Capabilities
- **Customer Navigation Header:** Home, Stores (Categories directory), Orders (Tracking), About, Notifications (with unread badge), Shared Cart Drawer, and Profile.
- **Marketplace Home:** Hero advertising banner space, top-order artisan stores, popular handcrafted items, and high-traffic categories.
- **Storefront Screen:** Dedicated artisan shop view featuring shop banner, verified rating, product catalog, and direct "File Complaint" action.
- **Shared Cart & Atomic Split Checkout:** Multi-store cart grouping, per-store subtotals, review & confirmation receipt modal, and split order creation.
- **Favourites & Wishlist Screen:** Save and manage favorite artisan products and bookmarked store workshops.
- **About & Policies Screen:** Artisan vetting standards, mission statement, terms of service, and return/refund policies.
