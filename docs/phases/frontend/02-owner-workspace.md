# Phase 16 — Store Owner & Staff 11-Section Workspace

## Objective
Provide an isolated, dedicated operational workspace for independent artisan store owners and their staff.

## Implemented Navigation Sections
1. `Dashboard`: Store gross sales, product counts, pending/fulfilled orders, and low-stock alerts.
2. `Products`: Full product lifecycle management, image URLs, pricing, and bulk actions.
3. `Inventory`: Real-time stock counters, minimum inventory thresholds, and manual replenishment history.
4. `Orders`: Fulfillment pipeline (Accept, Prepare, Dispatch with tracking number, Reject with reason, Print invoices).
5. `Offers`: Store-specific promotional discount codes and coupons with date ranges.
6. `Reviews & Complaints`: Merchant response composer for customer reviews, abusive review reporting to admin, and complaint dispute threads.
7. `Categories`: Browse platform categories and linked stores.
8. `Earnings`: Store gross sales, commission deductions (10%), net disbursable balances, and settlement history.
9. `Staff`: Provision store staff with granular permissions (`ORDERS_ONLY`, `PRODUCTS_ONLY`, `FULL_STORE_ACCESS`).
10. `Activities`: Store-scoped audit trail logging actions performed by the owner, staff, or platform admins.
11. `Settings`: Store profile customization (logo, banner, description) and submission/resubmission of store edit requests to admin.
