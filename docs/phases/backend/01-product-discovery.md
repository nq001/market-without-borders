# Phase 01 — Product Discovery & Multi-Store Scope

## Objective
Define **Market Without Borders (MWB)** as a multi-store artisan marketplace and distributed order management platform, prioritizing atomic multi-merchant checkouts and autonomous store workspaces over generic single-merchant catalogs.

## Target User Roles
1. **Super Admin:** Platform-wide oversight (Users, Stores, Categories, Side-by-Side Edit Requests, Orders, Complaints tribunal, Finance payouts, Reports, Activity Logs, Settings).
2. **Store Owner / Staff:** Independent artisan workshop workspace (Catalog, Inventory, Fulfillment pipeline, Store coupons, Review responses, Staff RBAC, Net earnings).
3. **Customer / Patron:** Multi-store storefront browsing, shared shopping cart across different stores, atomic split checkout with composite receipt review, disputes, and wishlist.

## Deliverables & Evidence
- Primary multi-store customer journey from discovery through review and complaint tribunal.
- Multi-tenant data segregation rules (products, orders, complaints, staff).
- Measurable reliability criteria: 100% atomic transaction boundaries and zero inventory overdrafts during flash sales.
